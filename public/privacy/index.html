Privacy

Effective 5 September 2026

Season Gap Garden stores garden records in this browser. It does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Bed names, crop and rest entries, dates, notes, crop notes, and season settings are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. During normal planning, the app makes no request beyond this site.

What leaves your device

The current release has no checkout. It has no advertising, behavioural analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Offline app storage

The installable app caches its interface after the first visit so it can work offline. Clearing browser data removes the cached app and local garden notebook.

Your controls

Export a season CSV or a complete JSON backup from Data. Delete individual records in the planner, or clear site data through your browser.

Questions

Contact privacy@sociobot.in. Do not email sensitive garden notes.
