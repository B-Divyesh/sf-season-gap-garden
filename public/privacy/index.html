Privacy, in plain soil

Effective 28 August 2026

Your garden records belong to you. Season Gap Garden is local-first and does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Bed names, crop and rest entries, dates, notes, crop templates, and season settings are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. We do not receive your garden records.

What leaves your device

The app does not currently offer checkout or make payment requests. It has no advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Offline and service worker storage

The installable app caches its interface files so it can work offline. Clearing this site’s browser data removes both the cached app and your local garden notebook. Download a full backup first if you want to keep it.

Your controls

You can export a season CSV or a complete JSON backup from the Data section, delete individual records in the app, or clear all site data through your browser.

Questions

Contact privacy@sociobot.in. Do not email sensitive garden notes.
